INTERACTIVE EARTHWORKS · SITE EDITION
A closer look at heavy machinery.

Drag the stage to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Camera views do not change the machine’s pose.

Hold Q/E for boom, W/S for stick, A/D for bucket, ←/→ for slew, and ↑/↓ for travel. Z gives quarter-speed arm precision. Click a slider to seek a position or drag continuously. Focus a slider and use left/right for 4° nudges.

Space toggles the brake; hold B for momentary braking. Shift toggles dynamics. 1/2/3 selects Arm/Travel/Assembly. J / L steers the tracks. F1–F5 selects Front/Left/Right/Rear/Top; O toggles orbit. ? opens help. Use the joystick and travel pad on touchscreens. R resets the arm; Return to start resets travel. Joint angles are local to their parent.

Site plan: switch between an overview of the yard and following the excavator. Dirt lanes, metre markers and track impressions show travel. Buildings, walls, and pile cores stop the chassis. Cones tip and slide; pipes roll when nudged by the chassis. Bucket contact and track edges deform stockpiles and shed loose soil. Reset Yard restores props and soil. These are lightweight effects, not full rigid-body or digging physics.

Assembly tab: separate the systems with the slider. Select a numbered part or click the model to inspect it. Reassemble & return restores your previous pose. Esc exits inspection.

Hydraulic lag, settling, braking and body rocking are lightweight simulated effects, not an engineering-grade physics model. Turn Dynamics off for a smoother showcase.
