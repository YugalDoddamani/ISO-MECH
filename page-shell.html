INTERACTIVE EARTHWORKS · SITE EDITION
A closer look at heavy machinery.

Drag the stage to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Camera views do not change the machine’s pose.

Hold an arrow to move a joint, or select its row and use your keyboard arrows. Body slew rotates the cab, not the camera.

Travel tab: W / S to drive, A / D to steer, Space to brake. Hold buttons on touchscreens. R resets the arm; Return to start resets travel. Joint angles are local to their parent.

Site plan: switch between an overview of the yard and following the excavator. Dirt lanes, metre markers and track impressions show travel. Major site obstacles stop the chassis; the boom is not collision-simulated.

Assembly tab: separate the systems with the slider. Select a numbered part or click the model to inspect it. Reassemble & return restores your previous pose. Esc exits inspection.

Hydraulic lag, settling, braking and body rocking are lightweight simulated effects, not an engineering-grade physics model. Turn Dynamics off for a smoother showcase.
